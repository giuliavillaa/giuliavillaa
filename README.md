## Giulia Villa

MSc student in Human-Centred Artificial Intelligence at DTU, Copenhagen.

What interests me is the seam between a model and the person on the other side
of it. My BSc in Artificial Intelligence covered both sides at once: machine
learning, computer vision and NLP, next to cognitive psychology, human-system
interaction, AI ethics and law. The master builds on that with interpretability
and human-AI interaction.

I’m interested in the engineering and system architecture required to make AI 
reliable where the stakes are high—from diagnostic pipelines in healthcare to
